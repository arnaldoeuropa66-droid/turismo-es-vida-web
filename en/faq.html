Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the retreat, medical companion, tours and everything else.

Retreat "Come back to you"

What exactly does the retreat include?

The retreat lasts 6 hours in a nature reserve and includes: purification with herb smoke and steam baths, Tibetan bowls, walking meditation, herbal tea, "tree of life" ritual (writing), fire circle, lunch and snack, mindfulness workshop, sound journey and closing mindfulness.

How long is it and where is it held?

It lasts 6 hours and takes place in a nature reserve near Cali. The exact location is shared once your booking is confirmed.

How many spots are there and how do I book?

Spots are limited to guarantee an intimate experience. To book, write to us on WhatsApp (+57 318 398 1089) or leave your contact in the chat form.

Do I need previous meditation experience?

No. The retreat is designed for people with or without experience. All practices are guided step by step and require no prior knowledge.

Medical Companion

What exactly is Medical Companion?

It is an emotional, logistical and wellness companion service for international travelers coming to Cali for medical procedures. It includes coordination of transportation, stay in recovery houses, emotional support, guided tours and nursing.

Does it include medical services?

No. Medical procedures (surgeries, treatments) are performed by independent doctors and clinics. Turismo es Vida SAS acts as a logistics and companion facilitator, not as a medical service provider.

In what languages do you provide the service?

We serve in Spanish and English. The main audience for this service is from the United States and Canada.

How does the process work step by step?

It has 4 phases: 1) Assessment (we listen to your needs), 2) Planning (transportation, stay, logistics), 3) Stay (emotional support during your recovery in Cali), and 4) Follow-up (virtual check-ins after you return home).

Tours and experiences in Cali

What tours do you offer?

We offer: Salsa Tour, Flavors and Culture City Tour (6 h), History and Culture AM/PM City Tour (4 h), Sonso Lagoon (kayak and birdwatching), Cloud Forest Km 18, and personalized celebrations (birthdays, anniversaries).

Can they be done in groups?

Yes. All tours can be booked for groups. We also offer corporate workshops with a holistic component.

Are the tours bilingual?

Yes, we offer tours in Spanish and English.
