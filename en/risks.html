1. Wellness activities and holistic retreats

Retreats and holistic therapies include practices such as meditation, breathing, Tibetan bowls, fire circles, steam baths, nature walks and writing rituals. These activities involve risks such as:

  • Intense emotional reactions derived from introspective processes.
  • Physical discomfort from prolonged postures, walking or temperature changes.
  • Exposure to smoke, steam or aromas that may affect people with allergies or respiratory conditions.
  • Risks inherent to natural environments (bites, falls, weather changes).

Complementary service, not medical. Turismo es Vida SAS does NOT provide medical services. Procedures are performed by independent doctors and clinics.

In this service, the client acknowledges that:

  • Medical risks of the procedure are the responsibility of the professional and the institution performing it.
  • Turismo es Vida SAS is not responsible for medical outcomes, surgical complications or diagnoses.
  • The support offered is emotional, logistical and companion-based.
  • The client must follow all instructions of their treating physician.

3. Tours and activities in Cali

Cultural, gastronomic, nature and salsa tours involve risks such as:

  • Falls, sprains or minor injuries during physical activities.
  • Allergic reactions to food.
  • Exposure to sun, heat or humidity.
  • Risks inherent to transport and travel.

4. Conditions you must report

You must inform us, before participating, if you have:

  • Pregnancy.
  • Heart, respiratory or neurological conditions.
  • Severe allergies.
  • Mental health conditions that may be affected by introspective practices.
  • Any other condition that may involve a risk.

5. Recommendations

  • Consult your doctor before participating if you have doubts.
  • Follow the team's instructions at all times.
  • Stay hydrated and rest properly.
  • Wear appropriate clothing and footwear.
  • Do not participate if you do not feel able.

6. Insurance

We recommend participants have medical or travel insurance covering the contracted activities. Turismo es Vida SAS does not offer medical insurance.
