This document explains the nature of the complementary therapies and wellness services offered by Turismo es Vida SAS. Please read it carefully before participating.

1. Nature of the services

The therapies and activities offered are complementary. They are not medical treatments, they do not diagnose, they do not cure and they do not replace professional medical care.

2. What they include

  • Relaxation and meditation practices.
  • Sound therapies (Tibetan bowls, sound journeys).
  • Conscious breathing practices.
  • Aromatherapy and steam baths (when applicable).
  • Symbolic rituals (writing, fire circle, tree of life).
  • Emotional support in wellness processes.

3. What they do NOT include

  • Medical diagnoses.
  • Prescription or administration of medications.
  • Treatment of physical or mental illnesses.
  • Replacement of psychological or psychiatric therapies.

4. Expected benefits

Participants often report feelings of relaxation, calm, mental clarity and general wellness. Results vary from person to person and no specific effect is guaranteed.

5. Risks

Although the practices are gentle, the following may occur:

  • Intense emotional reactions.
  • Mild physical discomfort.
  • Drowsiness or dizziness.
  • Allergic reactions to aromas or smoke (in sensitive individuals).

6. Participant's declaration

By booking and participating, I declare that:

  • I have read and understood this consent.
  • I understand that therapies are complementary and do not replace medical care.
  • I have reported relevant health conditions.
  • I assume the risks described.
  • I authorize Turismo es Vida SAS to provide the described services.
  • I understand I can withdraw at any time if I consider it necessary.

7. Confidentiality

The personal information you share will be treated confidentially in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

8. Revocation

You may revoke this consent at any time by writing to turismoesvida@gmail.com.
