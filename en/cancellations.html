Cancellation, Change and Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Legal notice: this English version is provided for information purposes. The Spanish version is the legally binding version for matters governed by Colombian law. Ver versión en español.
This policy complies with the Colombian Consumer Statute (Law 1480 of 2011) and seeks a fair balance between the client and Turismo es Vida SAS.

1. Right of withdrawal

Under Article 47 of Law 1480 of 2011, the client has the right to withdraw from the purchase within five (5) business days following confirmation of payment, provided that the service has not been rendered. In that case, the amount paid will be refunded without deductions or withholdings.

2. Cancellation by the client

When the client voluntarily cancels the booking after the withdrawal period, the following conditions apply:

Notice period Refund
More than 30 days before 100% of the amount (minus bank fees)
Between 15 and 30 days before 75% of the amount
Between 7 and 14 days before 50% of the amount
Less than 7 days before No refund (but the spot can be transferred to another person or date, see section 4)
No-show on the day of service No refund

3. Cancellation by Turismo es Vida SAS

If for reasons beyond our control we are forced to cancel a service:

  • Force majeure (extreme weather, health emergencies, road closures, etc.): the service will be rescheduled or 100% of the amount paid will be refunded.
  • Minimum participants not reached: 100% of the amount paid will be refunded or rescheduled, at the client's choice.
  • Other causes: 100% of the amount paid will be refunded.

4. Changes and transfers

The client may:

  • Transfer their spot to another person at no cost, notifying at least 48 hours in advance.
  • Reschedule to another available date, at no cost, notifying at least 7 days in advance.
  • Request a service change of equivalent value, subject to availability.

5. Medical and companion services

For Medical Companion services:

  • The medical services themselves (surgeries, treatments) are contracted directly with doctors or clinics and are governed by their own policies.
  • The logistics and emotional companion portion provided by Turismo es Vida SAS is governed by this policy.
  • If a medical procedure is cancelled or postponed for medical reasons, we will seek to reschedule the companion service at no additional cost.

6. Refunds

Refunds will be made:

  • By the same payment method used, when possible.
  • Within a maximum of 30 calendar days from approval of the request.
  • With applicable bank deductions, if any.

7. How to request a cancellation

Write to us at turismoesvida@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +57 318 398 1089 indicating:

  • Full name of the booking holder.
  • Date and service booked.
  • Reason for cancellation or change.
  • Refund details (if applicable).

8. Exceptions

Refunds do not apply in the following cases:

  • When the client cannot participate due to not meeting the indicated health requirements.
  • When the client voluntarily abandons an activity already started.
  • When the client engages in conduct contrary to community or safety rules.
  • When the service has been partially or fully rendered.
